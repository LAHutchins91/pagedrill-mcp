# The formatter vs. rivals (checked 2026-10-10, prices from each vendor's own page that day)

Our offer: **$5.99/mo or $49.99/yr**, free check-only report, cap 60 documents + 120 previews a month.
Lawrence's bar: cheaper AND does more. Legend: ✓ yes · ✗ no · ~ partial · ? not stated on their pages (not tested hands-on: testing needs uploads/accounts, not done).

## 1. Free-rival kill check (done first) — blunt answer: YES, free hosted whole-paper formatters exist

| Free tool | What it does (from its own page) | Does it kill us? |
|---|---|---|
| **MyClassHelp Assignment Formatter** (myclasshelp.com/assignment-formatter, also /apa-formatter) | "Free forever, no sign-ups." Paste your text, add sources by DOI/ISBN (Crossref), download a formatted .docx: title page/MLA header, margins, spacing, hanging indents, pagination. APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago 17, IEEE. Runs in the browser. | **Closest to a kill.** It does whole-paper formatting for $0. But it rebuilds a *new* document from pasted text plus sources typed into its form; it does not open the student's existing Word file, keep their tables/figures/footnotes, or convert the in-text citations they already typed (not stated on its page). No rule-by-rule sources. No military formats. |
| **mla-formatter.com** | Free, no signup, browser-only. MLA 9 layout: header block, running head, double spacing, margins, 0.5-in. indents, centered title; .docx import and export. | Kills a *layout-only* MLA product. Its page lists no Works Cited or citation handling. |
| **Formatly** (formatlyapp.com) | Uploads .docx, formats APA 7/MLA 9/Chicago 17/Harvard/IEEE/Turabian incl. citations, TOC; has an MCP server. Free plan: 3 jobs/month — but its full-site text says the free plan is *in-browser preview only, ".docx download requires Pro"* (its pricing.md contradicts this). Pro **$12/mo or $120/yr**, 50 docs. | Not free in practice (preview only). The strongest *paid* look-alike, and it is already in agent directories via MCP. |
| **Veermat** | Free format + preview + change list, no account; download needs Pro **$9/mo** (1,000 pages). APA 7/MLA 9/Chicago 17 layout; matches citations to entries but "never rewrites the text inside your citations". | Not free for the file. |
| **KitchenSink4Word** (free AGPL MCP, Glama) | 110-tool local Word MCP: Word-native citations/bibliography, Zotero, "publication style conversion (8 styles, beta)", validation, submission prep. Windows, local install. | Free for agent power users; no style profiles of page layout, no hosted flow for students. |
| Zotero MCPs, ReferenceMCP, word-footnotes-bibliography-mcp, mla-citation-generator-mcp | Citations/bibliographies only. | No. |
| ChatGPT GPTs (APA Wizard, Article APA Checker, Academic writing APA7) | Check/advise on APA; no formatted file promised on their listings. | No (check-only), but ChatGPT/Copilot can be asked to do it ad hoc. |
| Word built-in (References tab) | Bibliography from Word's own source list: APA (Microsoft's own page still documents an **APA 6th** fix), MLA, Chicago, IEEE, GOST, ISO 690; Microsoft warns formats may be out of date. No page-layout rules. Needs Microsoft 365 ($9.99/mo, $99.99/yr) or Office 2024. | No. |

**Conclusion:** we cannot be "cheaper" than MyClassHelp or mla-formatter.com, and our free tier (check-only report) gives less than theirs. We can only win on "does more": format the student's *existing* .docx in place, convert the citations they already typed, verified-and-cited rules, data checks against Crossref/PubMed, military formats, and availability inside ChatGPT/Claude/Grok. That pitch must be tested against MyClassHelp hands-on before launch (needs Lawrence's OK to upload sample files to a third-party site).

## 3. Every row a rival wins: fix + effort, or the reason not to

| Rival wins on | Fix (effort) or reason |
|---|---|
| **Price vs free tools** (MyClassHelp, mla-formatter.com, Zotero, KitchenSink4Word) | Can't beat $0. Fix: give the free tier **1 fully formatted download/month** on top of the check-only report so the free comparison isn't lopsided (S, 1–2 days; engine cost is cents per doc per cost_model.py). Pitch "your own file, your citations converted, every change shown" against rebuild-from-paste tools. |
| **PERRLA military price** ($79.95 × 50% = $39.98/yr < our $49.99) | Verified-military annual at $34.99 (S to build; needs a verification vendor account + Stripe price = Lawrence's approval). Otherwise we lose "cheaper" for service members. |
| **EndNote one-time** (~$150 student, used for years) | Strong reason: different job (library + Cite While You Write); our yearly price is lower than its upfront cost, and it doesn't format page layout. |
| **Style breadth** (Chicago, Turabian, Harvard at PERRLA/Formatly/MyClassHelp; thousands of CSL styles in Zotero/EndNote) | Buy access and verify: CMOS Online (Chicago 18/17, Turabian derives from it) and MLA Handbook Plus (finishes MLA 9). Each style after licensing: M (1–2 weeks incl. checklist + samples). Harvard: ship as "your university's guide" profile importer (M). Reason not to copy CSL: CSL covers citations only, not page layout. |
| **MLA 9 citation rules** (everyone else claims MLA 9) | Lawrence's copy is transcribed in `research/mla_answers/`. Implemented: 2.90, 2.106–2.109, 2.139, 5.7, 5.8, 5.89–5.91, 5.120–5.130, 6.5, 6.6, 6.28–6.30. Still unread, and not inferred: sec. 2.110, and title-within-a-title patterns not printed on pp. 74–76. The profile stays hidden. |
| **Vancouver** (Zotero/EndNote CSL) | Chapters 1–2 are read (print-PDFs in research/vanc_src/citing_medicine/) and the profile is selectable. Later chapters (internet, audiovisual, and the rest) are still unread. |
| **Reference library** (PERRLA, Zotero, EndNote) | Reason: not our product. Fix: import Zotero (via its API or .bib/.ris export) so refs come pre-structured (S, 3–5 days). |
| **Inside Word / Google Docs** (PERRLA, Zotero, EndNote, Word) | Google Docs share-link import like Veermat (S, 2–3 days). Word add-in = v2 (L; Microsoft Partner Center account needs Lawrence's approval). |
| **Tracked changes in the .docx** (Formatly, Scribbr) | Write text-level changes (citations, reference entries, labels) as real w:ins/w:del revisions; layout changes listed in a comment (M, 1–2 weeks). |
| **TOC** (Formatly, Scribbr) | Insert a Word TOC field built from the profile's heading styles (S, 1–2 days); needed for theses. |
| **Human review** (Scribbr) | Reason: different product at $2/page (a 10-page paper = $20, about 3 months of ours). |
| **NCO Kit: memos from scratch + NCOER/DA 4856/awards** | "New AR 25-50 memo from fields" tool on our verified profile (S, 2–3 days). NCOER/OER bullets and counseling forms: reason to skip — content writing, outside "format only"; NCO Kit is free for 10/month. |
| **Privacy: stays on device** (MyClassHelp, mla-formatter.com, Zotero, KitchenSink4Word) | Publish retention (delete uploads after 24 h, never train) and offer the engine as a local MCP for desktop agents (M, 1–2 weeks). |
| **Formatly's MCP + Chicago/Harvard/Turabian + tracked changes** | Our edge is verified, cited rules (Formatly's terms: "AI technology is inherently probabilistic"), Crossref/PubMed checks, IEEE conference template fidelity, military memos, lower price ($5.99 vs $12). Close the gaps above (styles, tracked changes, TOC). |
| **Class plans** (PERRLA $9.95/student/yr, Veermat course codes) | Course/education codes (S, 2–3 days) — Stripe coupon setup needs Lawrence's approval. |

**Net:** cheaper than every *paid* rival (Veermat $9, PERRLA $12.95/$79.95, NCO Kit $10/$97, Formatly $12/$120, Scribbr per page) except PERRLA's military price and EndNote's one-time model. Not cheaper than the free tools. "Does more" holds only for in-place editing + citation conversion + verified rules + Crossref/PubMed checks + AR 25-50; we currently do *less* on style count, Word/Docs integration, tracked changes, TOC and reference libraries.
