# Official style sources: reachability and verification status (checked 2026-10-10)

Note: where a deep URL could not be opened from the box, the table names the official site and says the exact path is not confirmed. Raw fetched pages are in apa_src/, mil_src/ and style_src/.

Rule: a style profile can ship only when its rules are tied to an official source we actually read. "Reached" = we fetched it from the box (or a GPO/official copy). Anything paywalled, licensed, or unreachable is marked **rules not verified — can't ship until licensed/verified**.
